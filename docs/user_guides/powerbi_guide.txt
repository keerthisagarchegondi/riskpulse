RiskPulse Power BI Navigation Guide

Audience
Executives, fraud strategy leaders, and risk operations managers reviewing strategic fraud performance.

Reports
RiskPulse Executive Fraud Analytics is the primary executive report. RiskPulse Fraud Trends Deep Dive supports deeper review of geographic, merchant, channel, and year-over-year trends.

Executive Summary Page
Purpose: show the health and financial value of the fraud platform.
Key visuals:
1. Total Fraud Prevented: total prevented amount over the selected period.
2. Fraud Rate: fraud transactions divided by total transactions.
3. Platform ROI: prevented fraud net of estimated operating cost.
4. System Uptime: availability signal from executive summary reporting.
5. Fraud Rate Trend: month-over-month direction.
6. Key Risk Indicators: high-risk volume, model AUC, false-positive rate, and SLA compliance.

Fraud Trends Page
Purpose: identify where fraud is growing and which business segments need attention.
Key visuals:
1. Fraud by Geography: filled map by ISO country code.
2. Fraud by Merchant Category: top categories by fraud amount and rate.
3. Fraud by Channel: online, POS, ATM, and mobile comparison.
4. YoY Comparison: annual fraud amount and rate movement.

Operational Metrics Page
Purpose: show whether fraud operations and model-serving systems are healthy.
Key visuals:
1. Average Alert Resolution.
2. Model AUC.
3. Pipeline Throughput per Hour.
4. System Uptime.
5. Alert Resolution Time by severity.
6. Model Accuracy over Time by model version.
7. Pipeline Throughput and latency.
8. SLA and uptime gauges.

Filters
Use date filters first, then tenant and channel filters. Tenant access is controlled by row-level security, so users only see allowed tenant rows.

Refresh
The dataset is configured for daily refresh at 06:00, 12:00, and 18:00 Eastern Time with incremental refresh. Refresh depends on Snowflake REPORTING tables being updated first.

RLS Validation
Use Power BI Service role testing to confirm tenant-restricted users can only see tenants assigned in SECURITY.POWERBI_USER_TENANT_ACCESS.
